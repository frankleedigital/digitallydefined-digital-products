Medicaid · The Map

  • VerifyAsset limits are state-specificMedicaid eligibility for long-term care depends heavily on your state's asset cap. Some states have a few thousand dollars of countable assets; others allow more. The planner's Medicaid tab shows your state's current threshold when you enter your state.
  • VerifyThe 5-year lookbackIf you transfer assets to qualify, Medicaid can look back five years. Document every gift. Some states have a Medicaid lookback exception for spouses; not all.
  • VerifySpousal protectionA married applicant can shelter the healthy spouse's home and a portion of assets. Single caregivers don't get the same spousal buffer — plan for a tighter squeeze.

FMLA · The Rules

  • 128 hours — that's itFMLA gives you 12 work weeks per year to care for a parent, child, or spouse. You get paid time only if your employer offers PTO on top — FMLA itself is unpaid.
  • Coverage thresholdYou qualify if you work 1,250+ hours/year and your employer has 50+ employees within 75 miles.
  • Not transferableYou cannot use FMLA on someone who isn't an eligible family member. A friend's parent, a neighbor's mom, a non-dependent parent — not covered.

State Paid Family Leave

  • Wage replacement, not your paycheckMost PFL states replace ~60–67% of your wages for a set number of weeks. California, New York, New Jersey, Washington, Rhode Island, Massachusetts, Colorado, and Connecticut all have PFL programs.
  • Stacks on top of FMLAPFL is paid; FMLA is unpaid. You can use PFL weeks while also holding FMLA status at the same time.
  • VerifyCheck your specific stateEach state's weeks, replacement rate, and eligibility criteria differ. The planner's PFL tab lists the state's current program when you enter it.

VA Aid & Attendance

  • Monthly supplement for caregiversVA A&A pays the veteran (or surviving spouse) extra compensation when in-home care or assisted living is needed. The current monthly supplement is a specific dollar amount — the planner pulls the current figure when you enter your state and eligibility.
  • The caregiver gets a bonusUnder certain conditions, an eligible caregiver may receive a small monthly care bonus on top of the veteran's A&A. This is separate from the veteran's benefit.
  • VerifyBenefit amounts change with cost-of-living adjustmentsAid & Attendance rates are adjusted annually. Use the planner's live lookup rather than memorizing the number.

Guardrail · Verify Before You File

Benefit eligibility, asset limits, and dollar amounts all change with state and with federal updates. The kit gives you the logic and the map. Before you file or transfer assets, run your specific case through a Medicaid planner or elder-law attorney in your state. This product is a planning framework, not legal advice.
