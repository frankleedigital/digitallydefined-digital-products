# Email Nurture System

For each lead magnet, a 7-email nurture sequence moves the subscriber from free value
to a low-ticket tripwire.

## Email 1: Welcome
**Subject Line:** "Here's your [Lead Magnet] + a friendly note"
**Purpose:** Deliver the lead magnet immediately and set expectations
**Main Takeaway:** "You're in. Here's your free resource. Let's get started."
**CTA:** "Reply with a '1' to get the paid version"

## Email 2: Quick Win
**Subject Line:** "Quick win #1: [Specific, actionable result]"
**Purpose:** Deliver immediate value and prove the transformation works
**Main Takeaway:** "Here's your first actionable win from this resource."
**CTA:** "Use this today, then reply '2' for the next step"

## Email 3: Mindset Shift
**Subject Line:** "Stop [common pain point] — start [desired state]"
**Purpose:** Reframe the problem and position the transformation
**Main Takeaway:** "This isn't just about [task] — it's about [outcome]."
**CTA:** "Reply '3' to see how this applies to you"

## Email 4: Case Study
**Subject Line:** "[Name] used this to [specific result] — here's how"
**Purpose:** Social proof and real-world proof of transformation
**Main Takeaway:** "This works. Here's exactly how someone else used it."
**CTA:** "Reply '4' to get the full system"

## Email 5: Product Introduction
**Subject Line:** "If you liked [lead magnet], you'll love [core product]"
**Purpose:** Introduce the core paid offer naturally
**Main Takeaway:** "Here's what you can do next to take this further."
**CTA:** "See the full system at [link]"

## Email 6: Offer
**Subject Line:** "[Offer name]: [one-line value proposition] — [price]"
**Purpose:** Present the tripwire/cheapest paid entry point
**Main Takeaway:** "This is the exact next step to go from free to paid."
**CTA:** "Claim yours at [secure link]"

## Email 7: Call To Action
**Subject Line:** "Last chance: [offer name] closes in [X] hours/days"
**Purpose:** Urgency and final conversion push
**Main Takeaway:** "Don't let this go. The next step is one click away."
**CTA:** "Claim your [offer name] now"
