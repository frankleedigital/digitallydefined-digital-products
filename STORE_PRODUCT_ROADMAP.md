# Store Product Roadmap

Convert every **Assessment, Calculator, Template, Training, and Prompt Pack** product into a
customer-facing storefront offer. Organized into three price tiers that match the
**DigitallyDefined** brand positioning and the **Gen X women building faceless digital real
estate** audience.

## Brand Positioning (for all tiers)

- **Audience:** Gen X women (45+) building faceless digital real estate — solo businesses,
  content engines, and service businesses that run without constant hands-on work.
- **Voice:** Clear, respectful, no-fluff, no-face-needed, step-by-step.
- **Promise:** "Turn what you already know into a real, cash-flowing digital business that
  works while you sleep."
- **Delivery standard:** Every product ships as an instant, downloadable, self-serve package.
  No calls, no project management, no hand-holding.
