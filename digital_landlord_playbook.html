PART ONE: THE DIGITAL LANDLORD MODEL EXPLAINED

What Is Rank and Rent?

Rank and Rent is a business model where you build a simple website, use Local SEO to rank it on Google for high-value local searches (like "Dallas Roofing Contractor" or "Miami HVAC Repair"), and then rent that website to a local business hungry for leads. You become a digital landlord — you own the asset, they pay the monthly rent.

Think of it like owning a billboard on the busiest street in town, but online. The business pays you every month to display their number — and you never touch the building.

Why This Model Creates Recurring Revenue

Unlike a one-time freelance project, Rank and Rent generates Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) — the gold standard of income. A single site rented for $1,500/month earns $18,000/year. Ten sites at that rate? $180,000/year — on autopilot.

The Gen X Advantage Is Real

  • Resourcefulness: Built-in problem-solving instincts beat any paid course.
  • Resilience: SEO takes patience — this model rewards those who don't quit in 30 days.
  • Organizational mastery: Running 5–10 sites is pure project management.
  • Relationship skills: Finding clients requires trust-building, not tech hype.
