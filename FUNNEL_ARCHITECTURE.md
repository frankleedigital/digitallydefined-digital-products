# Funnel Architecture

Complete funnel architecture for the DigitallyDefined ecosystem. Every paid product
has a lead-generating asset attached to it. Visitors enter through free value,
then progress naturally into paid offers.

## Funnel Sequence (Our Standard)

```
Traffic Source
   ↓
Lead Magnet (free)
   ↓
Email Opt-In
   ↓
Tripwire ($17–$47)
   ↓
Core Product ($97–$197)
   ↓
Premium Product ($297+)
   ↓
Agency Service
   ↓
Future SaaS
```
